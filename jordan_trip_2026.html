What to Bring & Wear

👟

Footwear

  • Sturdy walking shoes or trail runners — mandatory for Petra. The Siq and Monastery trail are long and uneven.
  • Sandals for the Dead Sea and camp evenings.
  • Flip-flops or slippers for the hotel pool and bathroom.
  • No heels, no fashion sneakers — Petra will destroy them.
👕

Clothing

  • Loose, breathable layers — September days are hot (30–35°C), desert nights are cool (15–18°C).
  • Long trousers or maxi skirts for Petra and Wadi Rum — modest and practical.
  • Light long-sleeve layer for the jeep tour (wind + sun).
  • Swimwear for the Dead Sea and Crowne Plaza pool.
  • One smart-casual outfit for dinner at the resort.
  • Avoid tight jeans — you'll be walking 8–12 km at Petra.
🌞

Sun & Heat

  • SPF 50+ sunscreen — reapply constantly at Petra and Wadi Rum.
  • Wide-brim hat or cap — essential, not optional.
  • Sunglasses — polarised preferred for the desert glare.
  • 1.5L+ water bottle — refill points are limited inside Petra.
  • Electrolyte sachets or tablets — the heat is deceptive.
🎒

Day Pack Essentials

  • Small daypack (15–20L) for Petra day — water, snacks, camera.
  • Cash (JOD) — many places don't take card, including some restaurants and small vendors inside Petra.
  • Portable power bank — long days, heavy camera/phone use.
  • Wet wipes — Wadi Rum is dusty; no amount of water helps.
  • Light snacks (nuts, dates) for the Petra walk — food inside is expensive.
💊

Health & Comfort

  • Any personal medication — pharmacies exist in Amman but not Wadi Rum.
  • Blister plasters — essential after Petra day.
  • Ibuprofen / paracetamol — feet and legs will complain by evening.
  • After-sun lotion for evenings at camp and the resort.
  • Insect repellent for Wadi Rum camp (mild but present).
📷

Camera & Tech

  • Camera or a phone that handles bright direct sun well.
  • Extra memory cards — you will fill one at Petra alone.
  • Universal travel adapter (Jordan uses Type B/D/G).
  • Download offline maps (Google Maps / Maps.me) — mobile data in Wadi Rum is minimal.
  • Jordan Pass app or downloaded PDF — have it ready at the gate.

Yallah — four days, three deserts, one sea.
